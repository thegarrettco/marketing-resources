The Pitch

The Garrett Companies is the most vertically integrated multifamily developer in America. For 26 years, we've refined a site-selection strategy that consistently puts us on the best dirt in every market we enter — and we build institutional-quality communities at a cost basis our competitors simply can't touch.

How? A construction platform built of career multifamily professionals, national-level subcontractor relationships, and our own internal subbase — when others were paying $1,500 per thousand board feet, we were locked at $350. That vertical control, from development through construction to property management, delivers the highest risk-adjusted returns for our investors and stakeholders. But the real engine is culture: in an industry with 30–40% turnover, we run at 6% — only 3% voluntary. Tactics win deals. Culture scales companies.
